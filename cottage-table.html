Beginner vegan pantry

What to keep in the cupboard when vegan cooking is new

You do not need a perfect pantry before you begin. Start with a few useful staples, then add flavour as you discover the meals you make again and again.

Start here

Buy for meals, then build the shelf

A useful vegan pantry is not a long list of worthy ingredients. It is a small set of things that can become soup, curry, pasta, noodles, bowls, toast toppings and quick suppers.

  • Choose two grains, two proteins, two sauces and five vegetables you actually like.
  • Keep one creamy thing, one sharp thing, one smoky thing and one savoury thing for instant depth.
  • Restock what you use often instead of buying every specialist ingredient at once.
01

Bases

These turn odds and ends into a real meal.

  • Rice, pasta, noodles, couscous or bulgur wheat
  • Oats, potatoes, sweet potatoes and wraps
  • Bread, pitta or flatbreads in the freezer
02

Protein

Keep these ready for quick bowls, stews and sandwich fillings.

  • Chickpeas, lentils, butter beans and black beans
  • Tofu, tempeh, vegan mince or sausages when useful
  • Peanut butter, tahini, nuts and seeds
03

Tins and jars

The cupboard helpers that rescue tired evenings.

  • Tinned tomatoes, passata and tomato puree
  • Coconut milk, stock cubes and curry paste
  • Roasted peppers, olives, pickles and jarred artichokes
04

Flavour makers

Use these to make simple food taste rounded and satisfying.

  • Soy sauce or tamari, miso and nutritional yeast
  • Mustard, vinegar, lemon juice and hot sauce
  • Maple syrup, brown sugar or dates for balance
05

Spices and shortcuts

A few jars can completely change beans, tofu or vegetables.

  • Smoked paprika, cumin, coriander, turmeric and chilli
  • Garlic granules, onion granules and mixed herbs
  • Black salt for an eggy note, plus favourite blends for smoky beans, stir fries and rice
06

Fresh and freezer

The everyday support that makes pantry cooking feel alive.

  • Onions, garlic, lemons, limes and fresh herbs
  • Carrots, greens, mushrooms and seasonal vegetables
  • Frozen peas, spinach, berries and chopped vegetables
01

Smoky beans

Beans, tinned tomatoes, smoked paprika, garlic and a spoon of tomato puree. Serve with rice, toast or a baked potato.

02

Quick coconut curry

Chickpeas or tofu, curry paste, coconut milk and frozen spinach. Finish with lemon or lime to brighten everything.

03

Comfort pasta

Pasta, lentils or vegan mince, tomatoes, herbs and nutritional yeast. Add mushrooms, courgette or greens if you have them.
