Bobbi’s teaching

You do not need to call yourself creative

You only need a starting point, a little time and permission to make something imperfect.

Bobbi’s workshops are designed for people who may not think of themselves as creative. Her teaching is gentle, encouraging and rooted in the belief that everyone can make something meaningful when given the right starting point.

  • Mixed-media art for beginners
  • Sacred Scraps using personal ephemera
  • Recycled and found-object art
  • Mindful making for wellbeing
  • Welcome Curtain workshops
  • Seasonal cottage projects
